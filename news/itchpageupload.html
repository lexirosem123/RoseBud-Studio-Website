Setting up a GOOD Itch.io page

July 14th, 2026

Working on a game is a process in itself... but I always struggle to make a good landing page for the work that I have put in on said game. Thankfully, Itch.io does make this a much easier process for developers, but that still requires me to put in some extra work to make the page look appealing.

I think this is the first time I have been happy with how my itch page turned out for a game, and I haven't even taken screenshots yet to put on the page with the desciption!

Hopefully my landing pages only get better from here, but I hope you enjoy the game and the itch page where it stands for now. I will be working on this game more after the judging period is over because I feel like it is a good idea for a game, I just need more time to make it truly perfect!
