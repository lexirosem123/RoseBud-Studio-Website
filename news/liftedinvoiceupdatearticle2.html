Meeting with the Client

June 12th, 2026

While working on this project, my main concern annd reasoning has been my father. I decided to make this application for him, and then seeing his encouragement I wanted to make it something bigger - something I can publish.

This past Monday I went over to his house to sit down and talk through what I have currently and what he thinks needs to be added or removed. It was an extremely informational meeting and I felt like I could see my project in a whole different light when watching him interact with it.

The plans right now are to focus on the UI, as there were some bugs. I also need to confirm that the Google API is working properly for new users. I am currently logged into my google calendar and it works when scheduling, but I couldn't get my dads account to log in just yet.

I am so excited to see where this goes and to get to the final stretch of developement - then it's just the process to publish and advertise my app!

Sign, Lexi
