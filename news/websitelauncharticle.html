I Launched My Very Own Dev Website

June 3rd, 2026

I finally stepped into the world of HTML and CSS, I had only previously dipped my toes in this pool but now I am diving in head first! For some context, I took the standard computer science elective in high school where they taught us the very basics of HTML and CSS. I fell in love with being able to create and design with just words on a computer screen!

Shortly after starting that class, my teacher suggested that I sign up for his college course on Python. He said I could dual enroll, the classes would be after school and I could get a head start on my education! I grew so invested in the programming field and dreamed of building my very own games. Unfortunately, most of the courses to do with programming in school are not for game development...

With this background, I knew a little about website design and structure, but had never taken the risk to create my own and learn how to host it. So here we are, over 7 years later, and I finally have my own website! I hope to continue to grow in my HTML/CSS strengths. Maybe I can make website design and hosting a part time job in the future!!

Thank you for listening to me, and supporting me/my dreams! I will do my best to keep you as up to date as possible!

Sign, Lexi
