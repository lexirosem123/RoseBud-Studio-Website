About RoseBud Studio

Alexis D.

Founder/Software Dev

Alexis is a solo indie developer with a passion for creating games and software. While she first discovered programming in high school, it wasn't until the COVID-19 pandemic that she fully immersed herself in teaching herself game development. Since then, she has spent countless hours learning, building, and experimenting with new technologies. By day, she works full-time as an insurance agent, and by night, she develops games and software projects under RoseBud Studio, pursuing her dream of becoming a full-time independent developer.
